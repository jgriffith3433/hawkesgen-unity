# Hawkes Gen for Unity

Make game assets from a prompt without leaving the Unity Editor: sprites with transparent backgrounds, animated sprite
sheets (sliced, with an AnimationClip), tileable PBR materials, skyboxes, sound effects, voice lines and music. You can
also edit images you already have: remove backgrounds, turn art into pixel art, or change them with words.

**Hawkes Gen is an online service.** Generation runs on Hawkes Gen's servers, not on your machine, and needs a free
hawkesgen.com account. It is paid with credits (1 credit = US$0.01). New accounts get 50 free credits; more are sold on
hawkesgen.com in packs ($10, $25, $100). Typical prices: an image about 5 credits, a sound about 8, a material about 10,
an animated sprite sheet about 50. Every job is priced by the service before it runs, and jobs that fail are refunded.

## Requirements

- Unity 2022.3 or newer (Built-in, URP or HDRP).
- An internet connection and a hawkesgen.com account (made during sign-in if you don't have one).
- Optional: the **2D Sprite** package (Window > Package Manager > Unity Registry > 2D Sprite). With it, sprite sheets are
  sliced into frames and get an AnimationClip automatically. Without it, you get the sheet and its frame data (JSON).

## Getting started

1. Open **Window > Hawkes Gen**.
2. Click **Sign in with your browser**. Your browser opens hawkesgen.com: sign in (or make an account) and approve
   "Unity Editor", choosing a daily spending limit. Go back to Unity; the window shows your account and credits.
3. Pick what to make, describe it, and click **Generate**. A progress bar shows each step, your place in line when the
   service is busy, and the time left. Results are saved into your project (default: `Assets/Hawkes Gen Output`) and
   selected in the Project window.

## What you can make

| In the window | What you get |
|---|---|
| Sprite or image | A PNG imported as a Sprite. "Transparent background" cuts the subject out. |
| Animated sprite sheet | From an image in your project and a motion ("run cycle facing right"): the character is cut out, animated, keyed to transparency and laid out as a sheet. With 2D Sprite installed: sliced Sprites and a looping AnimationClip for a SpriteRenderer. |
| Edit an image | Your image changed as described ("give him a golden sword"). |
| Remove background | A transparent PNG of the subject, trimmed. |
| Pixel art | Your image as pixel art: size, palette and an optional 1 px outline. |
| Tileable material | A seamless texture with normal and roughness maps, and a Material for your render pipeline. |
| Skybox | A 360° panorama and a Skybox/Panoramic Material (assign it in Lighting > Environment). |
| Sound effect, voice line, music | Audio files imported as AudioClips. |

## Your sign-in and your data

- Sign-in uses OAuth in your browser. The editor receives tokens through a one-time local address
  (`http://127.0.0.1:<port>/callback`) while you sign in, and stores them in **EditorPrefs** on this computer. They are
  never written into your project, your scenes or a build. **Sign out** in the window removes them. Manage or disconnect
  your editors, and change spending limits, at https://hawkesgen.com/connect.
- What is sent to Hawkes Gen: your prompts, and only the images you choose in the window (for editing, cut-outs, pixel art
  or sprite sheets). Nothing else from your project is read or sent. The package has no analytics.
- Hawkes Gen does not use your prompts, uploads or results to train models. Privacy policy: https://hawkesgen.com/privacy.
  Terms: https://hawkesgen.com/terms.

## AI disclosure

The assets this tool makes are generated by AI models running on Hawkes Gen's servers (image, video, audio and voice
models, chosen per tool). The sample image in `Samples/Knight.png` was generated with Hawkes Gen.

## Troubleshooting

- **Sign-in timed out:** finish the approval in the browser within 5 minutes, then try again. A firewall that blocks local
  connections to 127.0.0.1 also stops it.
- **"Not enough credits":** click **Buy credits** in the window (opens hawkesgen.com), then try again.
- **A daily limit was reached:** raise it for "Unity Editor" at https://hawkesgen.com/connect.
- **Sprite sheet not sliced:** install the 2D Sprite package, then use Generate again (or slice the saved sheet in the
  Sprite Editor using the saved JSON).
