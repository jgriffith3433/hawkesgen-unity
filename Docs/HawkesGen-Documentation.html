1. Overview

Hawkes Gen for Unity makes game assets from a prompt without leaving the Unity Editor: sprites with transparent backgrounds, animated sprite sheets (sliced into Sprites with an AnimationClip), tileable PBR materials, skyboxes, sound effects, voice lines and music. It also edits images you already have: remove backgrounds, turn art into pixel art, or change them with words. Everything is done from one window, Window > Hawkes Gen, and the results are imported into your project, ready to use.

2. Requirements

  • Unity 2022.3 or newer, with the Built-in render pipeline, URP or HDRP.
  • An internet connection and a hawkesgen.ai account (you can make one while signing in).
  • Optional: the 2D Sprite package (Window > Package Manager > Unity Registry > 2D Sprite). With it, sprite sheets are sliced into frames and get an AnimationClip automatically. Without it, you get the sheet and its frame data.

3. Costs and your account

Hawkes Gen is an online service. Generation runs on Hawkes Gen's servers, not on your computer, and is paid with credits from your hawkesgen.ai account (1 credit = US$0.01). New accounts get 50 free credits. More are sold on hawkesgen.ai in packs of $10, $25 and $100; credits don't expire.

4. Installation

  1. Import the package from the Asset Store.
  2. Everything is in Assets/HawkesGen: the editor code, this documentation, a sample image and the demo scene.
  3. Open Window > Hawkes Gen.

5. Signing in

  1. In the Hawkes Gen window, click Sign in with your browser.
  2. Your browser opens hawkesgen.ai. Sign in (or make an account), then approve Unity Editor.
  3. Go back to Unity. The window shows your account and credit balance.

Sign out in the window removes the sign-in from this computer.

6. Making assets, step by step

  1. In Make, choose what you want (for example Animated sprite sheet).
  2. For options that start from an image (sprite sheet, edit, remove background, pixel art), choose a texture from your project in Image. Selecting a texture in the Project window fills it in.
  3. Describe what you want. The typical cost is shown under the form.
  4. Click Generate. A progress bar shows each step, your place in line when the service is busy, and the time left. Click Stop to cancel; the unfinished step is refunded.
  5. When it finishes, the results are saved and selected in the Project window. Click a result's name to select it again.

8. Where results go and how they import

  • Results are saved to Assets/Hawkes Gen Output. Change the folder in the window's Save to field.
  • Images are imported as Sprites with transparency. Sprite sheets are set to Multiple and sliced from their frame JSON (a TexturePacker-style hash, also usable by other tools); the AnimationClip is saved next to the sheet. Drag it onto a GameObject with a SpriteRenderer, or add it to an Animator Controller.
  • Materials use the URP Lit, HDRP Lit or Standard shader, matching the project's render pipeline; the normal map is imported as a normal map.

9. The demo scene

Open Assets/HawkesGen/Samples/Hawkes Gen Demo.unity. It shows the sample sprite and the setup steps. Select the sample image (Samples/Knight.png), open Window > Hawkes Gen, choose Animated sprite sheet and try a motion such as "walking in place, a looping walk cycle".

10. Your sign-in, privacy and data

  • Sign-in uses OAuth in your browser. While you sign in, the editor listens on a one-time local address (http://127.0.0.1:<port>/callback) for the browser to hand back the approval.
  • The sign-in tokens are stored in EditorPrefs on this computer. They are never written into your project, your scenes or a build.
  • Only your prompts and the images you choose in the window are sent to Hawkes Gen. Nothing else from your project is read or sent. The package contains no analytics.
  • Hawkes Gen does not use your prompts, uploads or results to train models. Privacy policy: hawkesgen.ai/legal#privacy. Terms: hawkesgen.ai/legal#terms.

11. AI disclosure

The assets this tool makes are generated by AI models running on Hawkes Gen's servers (image, image-editing, video, sound, speech and music models, chosen per option). The sample image Samples/Knight.png was generated with Hawkes Gen. The package's code was written with AI assistance.

12. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Sign-in timed outFinish the approval in the browser within 5 minutes, then try again. A firewall that blocks local connections to 127.0.0.1 also stops it.
"Not enough credits"Click Buy credits in the window (it opens hawkesgen.ai), then try again.
The sheet wasn't slicedInstall the 2D Sprite package and generate again, or slice the saved sheet in the Sprite Editor using the saved JSON.

13. Script reference

No code is needed to use the package. For scripting, everything is in the HawkesGen.Editor namespace (an Editor-only assembly):

ClassUse
HawkesGenClientSignInAsync(); CallToolAsync(tool, args) calls a Hawkes Gen tool; RunAsync(tool, args, progress, cancel) runs it to the end with progress; UploadAsync(path) returns a file reference; DownloadAsync(url, path).
HawkesGenImporterAsSprite, AsSpriteSheet, AsMaterial, AsSkybox: turn downloaded files into Unity assets.
HawkesGenSettingsThe server, the output folder, sign-in state and SignOut().

Tool names and arguments are the Hawkes Gen MCP server's (hawkesgen.ai/mcp), the same tools Claude Code, Cursor and VS Code use.
